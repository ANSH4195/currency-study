# Trusted sources (answers must come from these only)

## Tier 1 — institutions and primary data
- World Gold Council — gold.org (gold demand, supply, history)
- LBMA — lbma.org.uk (London bullion market, prices, standards)
- USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries — pubs.usgs.gov (metal supply, production)
- Federal Reserve — federalreserve.gov (dollar, money history)
- Federal Reserve History — federalreservehistory.org (Fed's own essays: gold acts, 1971)
- US Mint — usmint.gov (coinage, gold/silver acts)
- Federal Reserve History — federalreservehistory.org (gold standard, 1933-34 gold acts, 1971; added by approval)
- Bank of England — bankofengland.co.uk (money history, banknotes)
- Royal Mint — royalmint.com (coinage history)
- Reserve Bank of India — rbi.org.in (rupee, gold reserves)
- BIS — bis.org, IMF — imf.org (monetary system, reserves)

## Tier 1 — raw data (preferred over articles)
- FRED (St. Louis Fed) — fred.stlouisfed.org (money supply, CPI, Fed holdings, debt series)
- BLS — bls.gov (US CPI, the official inflation data)
- Treasury FiscalData — fiscaldata.treasury.gov (federal debt, bonds, interest)

## Tier 1 — raw data (preferred over articles)
- BLS — bls.gov (US CPI inflation series)
- FRED, St. Louis Fed — fred.stlouisfed.org (money supply, Fed holdings, debt series)
- Treasury FiscalData — fiscaldata.treasury.gov (federal debt, bonds, interest)

## Tier 2 — museums and numismatic bodies
- Smithsonian (National Numismatic Collection) — americanhistory.si.edu
- British Museum — britishmuseum.org
- American Numismatic Association — money.org
- NGC — ngccoin.com, PCGS — pcgs.com (coin grading and history)
- Encyclopaedia Britannica — britannica.com (overview only)

## Tier 3 — community, corroboration only (never the sole source)
- CoinTalk — cointalk.com (metals and coin forum)
- Numista — numista.com (coin catalogue)
- Kitco — kitco.com (market prices, not education)

Rule: every answer cites its source; a claim needs a Tier 1/2 source, or Tier 3 plus one higher tier.

New domains: propose to the user first; add only after approval.
